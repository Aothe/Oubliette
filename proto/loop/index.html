            What is real here

            The prototype exists to play the loop and to prove the server can be checked. It decides nothing.

            Taken from the spec and the red team

            • A fixed 60 Hz tick in integers: positions in 1/256 px, a sine table, no floats in the state. The hash in the corner is the state; the same inputs give the same hash in any browser.
            • Every input is logged. Save a run and load it in another browser: the verdict says whether it replays to the same hash.
            • Rolls come from a per-tick stream seeded from a committed secret, never from a public block hash a bot could read first.
            • The boss door seals. Ready on the plate; those inside when it seals are locked in with the boss, scaled to their number; those outside are recalled with their wager and no loot.
            • Settlement is the lottery: finishers above a contribution bar share the pool and the fresh drops by a roll weighted by value wagered. The dead's recycled gear pays the next run, never the run it died in.
            • A run lasts at most ten minutes; then it halts where it stopped, deaths stand, the living leave with their wager and no loot.

            Still the slice's inventions

            • The class, the Turnkey and his phases, every enemy number, every item name and stat.
            • Loot you are carrying is left where you fall when you die.
            • The three skills and their items; Mend does not stack but has no cap.
            • Recall's numbers: three seconds, broken by any hit.

            Not in here

            • The chain, the exchange and other shards. The other delvers are bots with a reaction time.
            • Signed input batches and receipts: the log is unsigned.
            • Sound, touch controls, and the lottery's contribution formula beyond damage plus healing.
