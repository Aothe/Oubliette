          What is real here

          The slice exists to look at the game. It decides nothing.

          Taken from the spec

          • Top-down pixel art on 16-pixel tiles, in the Realm of the Mad God line.
          • One class, one dungeon, one boss, ten gear items across three tiers.
          • Death splits what you wagered: each piece has a one-in-three chance of returning to the boss pool, and burns otherwise.
          • The boss is a piñata. It drops fresh gear plus what the dead put in the pool.
          • One skill per delver, wagered and lost like any other piece.
          • You may recall before the boss, never at him. The loot is for those who lock in.
          • The boss door is sealed. Stand on the plate for three seconds: those on it go through and the door seals behind them; anyone else recalls with their wager. The Turnkey is measured to the head-count.
          • Every run starts in the Gatehouse: a strongbox, a fettering post, a ledger, the shard door, the Roll of the Forgotten and the mint. Nothing is at risk until the door seals, and nothing enters with nothing at stake.
          • Healing is potions, not regeneration: minted at the Mint or dropped by the Turnkey, carried into the run and at risk, drunk for a third of your health. Nothing heals for free in a fight.

          Invented for this slice

          • The class: a lantern-bearer whose light is your view. Class design is still open.
          • The Turnkey, his three phases, every enemy number, every item name and stat.
          • Loot you are carrying is left behind when you die. The spec has no rule for it yet.
          • Boss drops fall as bags to pick up. The spec's weighted lottery is not modelled.
          • The three skills and their items: Slip (Moth Feather), Mend (Moss Poultice), Sunder (Breaker's Maul). Minting one is free here.
          • Recall's numbers: three seconds, broken by any hit. It saves what you wagered and leaves what you carried, so only the seal pays.
          • The Gatehouse's shape and furniture, and the mint's prices (120 token + 0.002 ETH a set, 15 token a potion) are placeholders. The ledger lists nothing: there is no exchange here.
          • The potion's numbers: five in the pouch, a third of your health over two seconds, one at a time; no regeneration for four seconds after a hit or a shot, half a point a second after. The Turnkey drops three; the Forgotten, Shades and Gaolers drop one now and then.

          Not in here

          • The chain, the exchange and other shards. The three other delvers are bots.
          • Fixed-point determinism. This runs a seeded 60 Hz tick in ordinary floats.
          • Sound.
